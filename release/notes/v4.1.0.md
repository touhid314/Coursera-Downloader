Version 4.1.0 adds 1080p downloads and a new login option, and fixes several
problems reported on GitHub.

## New

- **1080p videos.** Choose 1080p to download videos in full HD. Few Coursera
  courses have 1080p videos; other videos are downloaded in the highest quality
  they have, usually 720p. (#18)
- **Paste login cookie.** A new login option for when the embedded browser and
  browser logins don't work: paste the CAUTH cookie from any browser where you
  are logged in to Coursera. (#14)
- The download summary now says how many quizzes and graded assignments were
  not downloaded. Coursera's current quiz format can't be downloaded, because
  its questions only appear after starting an attempt. (#10)

## Fixed

- One course item that Coursera answers unexpectedly no longer stops the whole
  course; it is reported and the rest of the course downloads. (#4)
- Reading the login from Brave or Firefox could crash the app; it now shows an
  error message instead. (#13)
- Malay subtitles could not be selected correctly. (#16)
- When the chosen resolution wasn't available, the app picked 720p even for
  videos that have 1080p.

See the [complete changelog](https://github.com/touhid314/Coursera-Downloader/blob/v4.1.0/changelog.md#v410)
for details.

## Downloads

- **Installer:** recommended for most Windows users.
- **Portable ZIP:** extract the full folder, then run `CourseraDownloader.exe`.
- **SHA256SUMS.txt:** checksums for verifying both downloads.
